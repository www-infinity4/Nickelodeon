# Nickelodeon 1986–1996

A synchronized classic-TV channel built on the same Infinity channel scaffold as the other live stations.

## Channel rules

- Focus: the 1986–1996 Nickelodeon era.
- Full episodes/programs only. Short clips are not used as substitutes.
- 48 half-hour television slots per local day.
- The lineup stays fixed during the day and refreshes at 12:00 AM in the viewer's local time.
- Programs play uninterrupted. If an episode ends before the half-hour, the channel holds on a station intermission until the next slot.
- If an embedded source becomes unavailable, the player shows an unavailable/intermission card instead of silently replacing it with a clip.
- The schedule is shuffled deterministically each day and interleaves shows to avoid long same-series runs.

## Current full-program pool

The initial 48-program pool includes:

- Double Dare
- Count Duckula
- Inspector Gadget
- Heathcliff
- Are You Afraid of the Dark?

Inspector Gadget and Heathcliff were produced before part of the target range, but they are included because they were part of Nickelodeon's late-1980s/early-1990s programming. The on-screen labels describe the Nickelodeon-era placement rather than falsely presenting those shows as newly produced in those years.

## Requested classics still being treated conservatively

Eureeka's Castle, The Noozles, Salute Your Shorts and other 1986–1996 favorites belong in the programming mix when a stable full-length embeddable source is available. This build intentionally does not fill those gaps with chopped clips, mislabeled videos, or questionable short uploads.

Embedded video availability can change at the source. The channel code is designed to fail cleanly when that happens.
